Understand the three doors

MCC runs All India Quota (50% of seats in state government colleges and central institutions), 100% of deemed university seats, and DNB seats. Your state runs the other 50% of its government seats plus private college seats. Register for both, since state rounds and MCC rounds overlap and you can hold a seat in one while upgrading in the other within the rules.

Branch first, or college first?

Pick a branch you will practise for thirty years. A strong branch at a mid-tier government college usually beats a weak-fit branch at a famous one. The exception: if your rank is borderline for Radiology or Dermatology, a newer government college can be the only realistic way in.

Fill choices generously

List 100 to 300 choices in true preference order: dream seats at the top, likely seats in the middle, and safe seats at the bottom. Allotment only moves you downward through your list, so a long list never hurts you, but a short one can leave you with nothing.

Deemed seats cost real money

Clinical deemed seats can cost ₹75 lakh to ₹1.4 crore for three years, plus a refundable security deposit that is forfeited if you exit after a certain round. Read the exit rules before locking a deemed seat, and compare that cost with a DNB seat at a good hospital.

Read the rounds, not just the cutoff

Round 1 closing ranks show where seats go when everyone is still choosing. By Round 3 (mop-up) and stray vacancy, clinical government seats barely move, but non-clinical and expensive deemed seats open up far deeper. Later rounds usually come with stricter rules on exit, upgrades and forfeiting your security deposit, so decide in advance how long you will wait.

Why 2023 looks strange

In 2023 the qualifying percentile was cut to zero after many seats went unfilled. Late-round ranks that year went unusually deep. PG Compass gives 2023 a lower weight in the 5-year average so it doesn't inflate your chances.

DNB is an equal degree

DNB is recognised as equivalent to MD/MS for teaching and practice. Top corporate hospitals give excellent hands-on exposure, often with lower fees and a decent stipend. Check the hospital's case load and whether it has had seats withdrawn.

Watch the bond

Many states require one to three years of government service after PG, backed by a bond of ₹10 lakh to ₹50 lakh. Factor this into your plans if you want to go abroad or pursue a super-speciality immediately.

INI-CET is separate

AIIMS, PGIMER Chandigarh, JIPMER and NIMHANS admit through INI-CET, not NEET PG, so they do not appear here. If you wrote INI-CET, run that counselling in parallel.

Keep your documents ready

Internship completion certificate, NMC or state council registration, category and domicile certificates in the prescribed format, and the NEET PG scorecard. Missing a single original at reporting can cost you an allotted seat.

All ranks, fees, stipends and bond figures in PG Compass are an indicative model based on broad historical patterns. They are not official data. Always verify with mcc.nic.in, natboard.edu.in and your state counselling authority before filling choices.
